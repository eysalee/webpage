Applied Cryptography Final Project

Fall 2026
Barnard College


The final project for Applied Crypto is a chance for students to explore a cryptography or security topic of their choice.

Project Milestones and Deadlines

  1. Project Proposal: Due Friday, Nov 6 at 11:59pm
    Each student will choose a topic and conduct a (short) literature review of their selected area. They will identify what they hope to achieve at the end of the project and preliminary plan for achieving their identified deliverables. The deliverables for each project may vary, and students should not hestitate to seek feedback early on potential projects. As a general rule of thumb, projects with less involved implementations should expect to dedicate a larger portion to background and literature.
    Students will submit a their proposals via email and must receive approval on their proposal. See Project Proposal Assignment for details.
  2. Progress Report: Due Monday, Dec 7 at 11:59pm
    For the progress report, students will state what progress they have made towards their deliverables. The progress report is primarily graded on completion.
  3. Final Presentations: Due Monday, Dec 7 at 2:40pm
    Each project will be presented to the rest of the class starting Monday, December 7. Copies of all presentations are due at the start of class on the first day of presentations. The amount of time will be around 20 minutes. We will aim to complete all presentations December 7 and December 9, but may extend presentations to the final day of classes (December 14) if necessary.
  4. Final Project Deliverables: Due Wednesday, Dec 16 at 11:59pm
    Students will submit their completed final project via Gradescope.

Final Project Grading Breakdown:
  • Project Proposal - 10%
  • Progress Report - 30%
  • Final Presentation - 30%
  • Deliverables - 30%
